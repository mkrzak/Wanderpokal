The wandering cup

About

Wanderpokal is a German word that translates literally to “wandering cup.” The idea is simple: a trophy is awarded to the winning team and passes from the reigning champions to the next winners each time the competition is held.

But there’s more to it than just the trophy! The winners earn a special prize: a guaranteed spot in the next Wanderpokal session. That makes every match worth fighting for!

Over time, some teams managed to win the trophy several times in a row. To keep the competition exciting and give every team a fair chance of winning, we introduced a rule: a team can win the Wanderpokal trophy a maximum of three times.

Every year on 5 November, we celebrate the anniversary of Wanderpokal with a cake, celebrating the wonderful friendships and the strong sense of community that bring players together across teams and clubs.

What the columns mean

#
Position in the table as it is currently sorted.
Nights
Tuesdays played that have full scores, so the night's 1st, 2nd and 3rd places are known.
Titles
Nights your team finished 1st (won the night). Titles + 2nd + 3rd = Nights.
Win %
Titles ÷ Nights.
2nd · 3rd
Nights your team finished second or third.
Sets W · Sets L
Sets (the 15-minute games) your team won or lost.
Set win %
Sets W ÷ all sets played.
Pts / set
Average points your team scored in a set.
+/−
Points your team scored minus points scored against it, over all sets. Above 0 means you outscored the opposition.
