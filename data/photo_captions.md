# Wanderpokal photo captions

For each photo, put the names in order as people stand **left to right**, then change `[ ]` to `[x]`.
Only ticked lines appear on the website, as "Left to right: …". Unticked lines keep the usual winners list.

- Names are pre-filled from that night's winning team (match sheet order). Add, remove or fix names as needed.
- Leave a line unticked if you're not sure.
- Keep the file name and the `|` separators as they are.
- After saving, rebuild: `python3 scripts/build.py ../Wanderpokal.xlsx`
